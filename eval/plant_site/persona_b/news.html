Energy Leaders Clash at Davos 2023: Okonkwo Criticizes Western Climate Mandates

Financial Times, January 2023

Marcus Okonkwo, Chairman of Okonkwo Energy Holdings, sparked controversy at the World Economic Forum in Davos last week when he argued that Western-imposed climate transition timelines were "economically colonist" and unfair to African energy producers. His remarks drew sharp criticism from European and North American climate advocates.

The comments have attracted scrutiny from some of Okonkwo Energy's European investor base, though the company has not indicated any investor withdrawal.
