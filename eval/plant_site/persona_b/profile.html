Marcus Okonkwo

Co-Founder and Chairman, Okonkwo Energy Holdings

Biography

Marcus Okonkwo co-founded Okonkwo Energy Holdings in 2008, building it into one of West Africa's largest independent energy companies. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Engineering from the University of Lagos (1999).

Mr. Okonkwo serves as director of three UK-registered subsidiaries, including Okonkwo Energy UK Ltd, registered at Companies House.

Regulatory History

In 2020, Okonkwo Energy Holdings underwent preliminary OFAC sanctions screening as part of a US counterparty due diligence process. The screening was completed with no sanctions designation. The company has no current OFAC listings.

Between 2015 and 2019, Mr. Okonkwo maintained a business partnership with Victor Adeyemi through a joint venture in the Niger Delta. Mr. Adeyemi was designated by OFAC in November 2019. Mr. Okonkwo terminated all business relationships with Mr. Adeyemi prior to the designation date.
