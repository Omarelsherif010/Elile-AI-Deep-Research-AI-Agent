Alexandra Chen's GreenTech Ventures Linked to Hartwell Group Fund

Singapore Business Review — March 2023

GreenTech Ventures, co-founded by Alexandra Chen in 2019, received co-investment from a fund managed by Hartwell Group, according to regulatory filings reviewed by Singapore Business Review. The fund connection was disclosed in GreenTech's Series A documentation.

Additionally, in 2022, GreenTech Ventures received a preliminary inquiry letter from the US Securities and Exchange Commission regarding its cross-border fundraising activities. The company stated the matter was resolved without penalty.

Ms. Chen did not respond to requests for comment.
