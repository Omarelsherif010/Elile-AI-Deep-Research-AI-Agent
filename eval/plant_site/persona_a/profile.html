Alexandra Chen

Chief Executive Officer, Meridian Capital Partners

Biography

Alexandra Chen serves as Chief Executive Officer of Meridian Capital Partners, a Singapore-based alternative investment firm managing over USD 2.3 billion in assets under management.

Prior to founding Meridian Capital Partners, Ms. Chen served as Managing Director at DBS Bank from 2012 to 2018, where she led the bank's structured finance division in Southeast Asia.

Ms. Chen holds a Master of Business Administration from INSEAD (2009) and a Bachelor of Commerce from the National University of Singapore.

Board Memberships and Advisory Roles

  • Board Member, Singapore FinTech Association (since 2020)
  • Advisory Board, GreenTech Ventures (co-founder, 2019)

Regulatory Disclosures

In 2021, the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) conducted an enforcement inquiry into Meridian Capital's trading practices. The inquiry was resolved in Q4 2021 with no enforcement action taken. Ms. Chen cooperated fully with the review.
